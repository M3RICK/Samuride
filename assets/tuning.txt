# Samuride tuning: the game's balance, in one file.
#
# Change a number, save, restart the game. Delete a line to get its default back.
# Distances are in tiles: a runner is 2 tiles tall, a tile is about a metre. Times are in seconds.
# In versus, the host's file decides what happens; the other player only draws with theirs.
# While a value isn't at its default, the game says CUSTOM TUNING in a corner.
# Testing something? Send this file with what you felt: it says exactly what you played.
#
# Words used below:
#   tile       the grid the map is drawn on: a square about a metre wide. The screen is 10 tiles
#              tall and 20 wide; a runner is 2 tiles tall. The distance on screen counts tiles.
#   row        a line of tiles across the screen, from top to bottom: 10 rows. Column: the same, upright.
#   chunk      the map is made 40 tiles at a time, as you run, each piece drawn at random: a chunk.
#              So 3 chunks is 120 m, 30 chunks is 1200 m.
#   chance     from 0 (never) to 1 (always): 0.2 is one time in five.
#   leader     the runner furthest ahead (in solo, you).
#   cooldown   seconds before you can attack again.
#   reach      how far ahead a melee attack lands. Radius: how close to a shot's line a drone gets hit.
#   grace      a moment of invincibility, to get clear after a shield breaks or a power-up ends.
